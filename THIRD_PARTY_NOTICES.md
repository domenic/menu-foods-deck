The repository's MIT license doesn't cover the cards' photos and diagrams. They come from Wikimedia Commons, under licenses chosen by their authors, and appear in:

- the released deck packages, where each card links to its images' Commons pages, which name each image's author and license; and
- the screenshots in `screenshots/`, which show the images of the cards they depict, named in those cards' `image` and `served_image` fields in `menu-foods.yaml`.
